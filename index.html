    Tips for riding with it
    • Add it to your home screen (iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen; Android: the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen). It opens full screen and your rides are safer from browser clean-ups.
    • Keep the app open with the screen on while riding. Phone browsers pause GPS when the screen locks, so the app asks the phone to stay awake. A handlebar mount helps.
    • Your ghost is the ride you pick before you start. By default it's your most recent ride that starts near you; PB marks your fastest on that route.
    • Rides are kept on this phone only. Export a GPX now and then to back them up or upload them to Strava.
    • Import GPX from Strava or Garmin to race an old ride. It needs a recorded activity with timestamps, not a planned route.
